    Content goes here.
  </div>

Prefixes apply at and below their breakpoints: l- at 60em, m- at 45em, and s- at 30em. Without an s- override, columns become full width on small screens.

Typography

Headings and paragraphs follow a 1.5 baseline rhythm. The stylesheet also includes empty rules for links and form controls.
